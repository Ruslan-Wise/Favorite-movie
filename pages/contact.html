Some interesting facts about the film "Knockin' On Heaven's Door".


    The original title was borrowed from the song of the same name by the famous musician Bob Dylan, which, incidentally, became the soundtrack for the film.

    The names of the main characters were not chosen at random. Martin Brest was named after the famous director who made the film Scent of a Woman, and Rudy Wurlitzer is the name of the screenwriter who worked on Bertolucci's film Little Buddha.

    The film took a minimum amount of time to shoot — only about a month. The reason was the low budget — around €2 million. Interestingly, the idea for the advertising poster came from Quentin Tarantino.

    There is quite a lot of advertising for Hell optics in the film, as this company was one of the sponsors.

    The film combines several genres. It begins as a comedy, then turns into an exciting action film, and ends as a true drama.

    For the film, the original 1956 Cadillac Coupe Deville had to be repainted pink, as this was the exact model of car Elvis Presley gave to his mother.

    Another feature of the film is that all the characters act in pairs: Martin and Rudy, two gangsters, two mafiosi, and two sides pursuing the fugitives: the police and criminals.

    After its release, the film became very popular — more than 3 million viewers watched it in Germany.

    Today, this film ranks 7th in the rating of 500 favourite films.

    In 2009, a remake of this film was released in Japan under the title "Heaven's Gate". Its main characters were a 28 year old man and a 14 year old girl.

    Fans of this film like to repeat the phrase: "People fall into two categories: those who like this film and those who haven't seen it."
